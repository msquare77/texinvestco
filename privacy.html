Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your information.

Our Commitment to Privacy

TexInvestCo is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard it.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Company
  • Job title (where provided)
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Details of your inquiry or message

We only collect information that is necessary to respond to your request or provide our services.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Evaluate potential business engagements
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations where applicable

TexInvestCo does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We may share information with our operating platform, Murkez Technologies, solely to support the delivery of services described on this site.

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use standard analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with our content and improve the overall user experience. Analytics information is collected in an aggregated and anonymized form wherever practical. We do not use cookies or tracking technologies for behavioral advertising.

Information Security

Protecting client and business information is fundamental to how we operate. Our operating platform, Murkez Technologies, maintains internationally recognized management systems including ISO 27001 (Information Security Management) and ISO 9001 (Quality Management), and applies those standards across the operational environments it manages on behalf of TexInvestCo engagements.

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect the information entrusted to us.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. TexInvestCo is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites, and we encourage visitors to review their privacy policies.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, services, or legal and regulatory requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.
