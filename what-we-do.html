How the Capital Is Structured

Aligned Incentives,
Shared Risk

We fund the Murkez operating teams we deploy, covering payroll and delivery costs, while taking equity participation and, where appropriate, structuring convertible debt. Direct capital is deployed where working capital is required.

This keeps incentives aligned, shares risk, and ties our outcomes to the founder's success.

If the build does not move forward, TexInvestCo absorbs the cost of the teams deployed during the agreed build phase. If it succeeds, we share in the upside together.
